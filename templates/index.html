Why Sponsor Colchester Rangers FC?

  • Reach a growing local football audience across social media, community events, and club communications.
  • Build brand awareness with a strong community-focused identity in Essex.
  • Support grassroots football while giving your business a visible local presence.
  • Align with a club that values teamwork, community, and professional development.

What we offer in return

  • Logo placement on sponsored kit, training wear, or club materials where agreed.
  • Social media mentions and promotional shout-outs across our channels.
  • Recognition on the club website and sponsor sections, with a link to your website, and your logo on every website page.
  • Opportunities to connect with players, supporters, and local partners through club events.
